## OpenEvidence preferred shares

We are buying approximately US$10 million of OpenEvidence preferred shares. We prefer a direct secondary transfer of those shares. Our target implied valuation for the company is approximately US$12 billion.

The US$12 billion figure is the valuation we are using as a buying target. It is not OpenEvidence's current market valuation, and it is not the valuation from its latest financing. A price per share depends on the share class, the rights on those shares, the current capitalization, and the terms we agree.

To continue the conversation, use the form on our OpenEvidence page: [https://bas1s.ventures/secondaries/openevidence](https://bas1s.ventures/secondaries/openevidence). That page was last reviewed on 19 November 2026.

## Who should write

Write to us if you hold the preferred shares yourself, or if you are authorized to act for the entity that holds them. A company or fund that itself holds the OpenEvidence shares can sell those shares. Selling an interest in that holding vehicle, rather than the shares, is a different transaction, and it is not the one we are seeking.

We are not inviting brokers, intermediaries, or introducers.

## What a purchase still depends on

This is our own buying interest. We are not affiliated with OpenEvidence, and OpenEvidence has not endorsed this request.

No purchase has been agreed or completed. Any purchase still depends on diligence, an agreed price, the share transfer rules, any right of first refusal, company consent, and final documents. We are not saying that the company has approved a transfer.
