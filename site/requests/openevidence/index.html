Public request

Buying OpenEvidence preferred shares

BAS1S Ventures has published that it is buying approximately US$10 million of OpenEvidence preferred shares, preferably through a direct secondary share transfer, at a target implied company valuation of approximately US$12 billion.

Direct shareholders are invited to contact Wonder Xu at Bas1s Ventures. This page restates the public mandate for this index. It is not a statement that a purchase has been agreed or completed.

Limits of this request

What the valuation figure means

The US$12 billion figure is the indicative acquisition valuation target stated on the source page. It is not a statement of OpenEvidence's current market valuation or latest financing valuation. A price per share depends on the share class, its rights, the current capitalization and the agreed terms.

Who the mandate invites

The source page invites direct shareholders and authorized personnel of an entity that directly holds the shares. It says a shareholding vehicle that itself holds the underlying OpenEvidence preferred shares may act as a direct seller, and that selling interests in that vehicle is a different structure which this mandate does not invite. Brokers, intermediaries and introducers are not invited.

What this request does not say

The source page calls this an independent acquisition interest and states that BAS1S Ventures is not affiliated with or endorsed by OpenEvidence. Any purchase remains subject to diligence, agreed pricing, applicable transfer restrictions, rights of first refusal, company consent and definitive documentation. Company approval is not represented. This site does not promise that a transfer will close.

Contact pathway

The contact pathway is the original mandate. That page is where BAS1S Ventures asks direct shareholders to start a confidential discussion. It publishes the contact below and its own form. This site has no form and does not collect names, addresses or financial information.
